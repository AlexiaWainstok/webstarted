# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS clickable prototype (user: "hacelo prototipado"). No build step; screens are linked pages so the flow can be walked end to end.

## Users

Primary: tech professionals in Latin America (backend, frontend, fullstack, AI & data, QA & automation, DevOps & cloud, management/leads) looking for a 100% remote role at a US startup, paid in USD. They read Spanish; many are mid-career and evaluating whether their English and profile are "enough". They arrive either from a job post or from word of mouth and decide in minutes whether to invest time in a profile.

Secondary (indirect): Webstarted recruiters and US startup clients, who see verified profiles.

## Product Purpose

Webstarted Talent Network validates a candidate's technical profile and English once, then presents them directly to US startup clients. Success: a visitor completes a verified profile (basic data → experience → 30–60s English video) and enters the selection process.

## Positioning

Verify once, get presented many times: one profile plus one short English video pre-verifies level (B2/C1) before any client sees the candidate, instead of re-applying per job. Webstarted Academy adds a "Verified Junior / Academy Certified" badge that gives priority with recruiters.

## Operating Context

Selection process, 5 steps (confirmed): 1. Postulación (create verified profile) · 2. Video en inglés (pre-verification of English level) · 3. Tech fit interview (with Webstarted's team) · 4. Entrevistas con el cliente · 5. Oferta en USD (100% remote role).

Surfaces: Home (job searches, Talent Network, Academy, #WorkWithWebstarted community), job listings (Búsquedas laborales), Talent Network 3-step registration form, Academy, Community. Profile completeness is tracked and the product tells the user exactly what is missing (e.g. "video corto en inglés · CV en inglés · GitHub o portfolio"). Progress is saved if the user leaves mid-form. Video can be recorded from camera or uploaded.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Job areas: Backend & Go/Python, Frontend, Fullstack, AI & Data Science, QA & Automation, DevOps & Cloud, Management / Leads.
- English levels referenced: B2, C1.
- Seniorities in use: Junior, Semi Senior, Senior.
- Undecided: real client logos (placeholders until provided); exact palette values from webstartedacademy.com (host blocked in this environment; pending).

## Brand Commitments

- Name "webstarted" with the "TALENT NETWORK" sub-mark; the dotted logo mark is fixed (user: "logo fijo").
- Palette: binding source is https://www.webstartedacademy.com/ (user, 2026-11-03). The old prototype's colors (Tailwind slate-900 / violet-600 / sky-400) are not the brand and are not to be reused.
- Copy in Spanish, neutral "tú" register, as in the existing prototype. Hashtag: #WorkWithWebstarted.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed publishable: "Más de 91,000 profesionales registrados"; the 5-step process; the Academy badge "Verified Junior / Academy Certified" and its priority effect.
Not confirmed, do not publish as fact: client logos, testimonials, named hires, salary figures, number of startups placed, investor/accelerator affiliations. Candidate cards and stories are synthetic demo content and must be labelled or obviously placeholder.

## Product Principles

1. Respect the candidate's time: say exactly what's needed and what's missing, never a generic rejection.
2. One verification, many opportunities: every screen reinforces that the profile is reusable.
3. Honest process: show all five steps and where the candidate stands.
4. English is a gate, not a mystery: make the video step feel doable and explain why it exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish-first copy; forms must be fully keyboard-accessible with visible labels and specific error messages; respect reduced motion.
