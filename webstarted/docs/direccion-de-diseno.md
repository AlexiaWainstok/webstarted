# Surface brief: Home + Registro Talent Network

Scope: `index.html` (Home, Persuade) and `registro.html` (3-step Talent Network registration, Operate inside the same world). Static clickable prototype.
Audience/job: LatAm tech professional deciding whether to invest 3 minutes in a verified profile. Action: start the registration (pick area → board). Proof: 91,000+ registered professionals, the real 5-step process, Academy badge. Constraints: logo fixed; palette must come from webstartedacademy.com (pending: host blocked, tokens isolated in one `:root` block); client logos are placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The selection process is a transit line and the candidate is a passenger: five stations from Postulación to Oferta en USD, with an "Estás aquí" marker that moves with the profile. Refuses the category default of split hero + card grid + a separate numbered step row.

OWN-WORLD: Latin American metro wayfinding (pictogram station system, enamel line signage). One thick brand-coloured line with white roundel stations, geometric station pictograms, solid sign plates with white grotesk type, small line badges, tight corners, a light daytime ground with dark sign-plate panels. Each product area is a line with its own colour on a network map.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole route in one look, understands the English video is a station, not a wall, that the profile is boarded once and reused, and boards from station 1.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: headline top-left; a full-width horizontal line crosses the viewport at ~60% height with five stations, pictograms and names; station 1 holds the working boarding control (area select + "Subir a la línea" CTA → registro with area prefilled); "Más de 91,000 profesionales registrados" as a platform sign at the right. Registro: vertical branch line at left with 3 stations, form panel aligned to the active station.

FORM: transit wayfinding system, #1 on the ordered list (chosen by the user over the rolled assignment, security credential); seed:871ebc866f139265. Signature interaction: the "Estás aquí" train dot travels along the line between stations as steps complete (transform only, ease-out, ~280ms, instant under reduced motion); stations on Home open a "próxima estación" detail sign on hover/focus.
